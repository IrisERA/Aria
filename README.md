ARIA
I watch a lot of movies and series, and I kept losing track of what I'd seen, what I loved, and how many times I'd rewatched my favourites. The apps that already do this are either packed with ads or hide the good stuff behind a paywall. So I built ARIA.

ARIA is a free, ad-free place to keep track of everything you watch. No tracking, no upsells, no feed trying to keep you scrolling.

What it does
Search anything. Movies and series, with posters and descriptions. It understands how people actually type: "dune part 2", "dune part two" and "toy story 3" all work.
Keep count. Log what you've watched and how many times. Rate it from ½ to 5 stars, star your favourites, or park it on your Watchlist for later.
Make it yours. Drag titles into your own order. Build custom lists with notes, and jump around them with a number filter.
See your taste. Group by series (Dune, Toy Story and so on) in colour-coded groups, or by genre. Filter by genre whenever you want.
Never lose your list. Sign in with Google or GitHub and your list follows you to any device. Deleted something by accident? It sits in "Recently deleted" for 30 days.
Use it anywhere. There's a roomy desktop layout and a compact phone layout, and you can install it on your phone like an app.
Skip the account if you like. ARIA works as a guest too. Your list just stays on that one device.
What's next
Episode tracking for series, "where to watch" links, friend sharing and trailers, plus a desktop app that can notice what you're watching and log it for you. Recommendations are coming too, built to help you find something good, not to keep you hooked.
