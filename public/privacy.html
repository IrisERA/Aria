Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

ARIA is free and has no ads. We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

What we store

  • Your watching data: the titles you add, ratings, watch counts, lists, notes, comments and settings.
  • If you sign in: your name, email address and profile picture from Google or GitHub, so we can show your profile and keep your data in your account.

Where it's stored

If you don't sign in, your data stays in your browser on your device. If you sign in, it's also stored in your private account on Supabase, our database provider, so it syncs between your devices. Only you can read your account's data.

Other services ARIA uses

  • Google and GitHub, to sign you in.
  • Supabase, to store your account and synced data.
  • IMDb, iTunes and TVmaze, to search for titles. Your search words are sent to them, but not your name or account.
  • Vercel Web Analytics, to count visits to the website (for example which pages are opened and roughly which country visitors are in). It uses no cookies and doesn't identify you.

Your choices

  • Download everything with Settings → Privacy & data → Download my data.
  • Delete titles at any time. Removed titles are kept in "Recently deleted" for 30 days, then removed.
  • To delete your account and all synced data, email us at aria.app.help@protonmail.com.

Children

ARIA isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we'll let you know in the app.

Contact

Email aria.app.help@protonmail.com.
